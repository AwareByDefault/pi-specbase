## Outcome

One board action serially reviews a selected ready change, implements its declared enforcement sources, implements tasks until native evidence is green, reviews and fixes local refactor opportunities, and leaves scoped atomic commits with a resumable audit trail.

## Demonstration

A fixture repository reaches a clean, green local commit through bounded fail-fix loops; failures halt with stable recovery state and never advance a successor stack member.

## Explicit deferrals

The Specbase review panel, remote push, draft PR creation, merge, and archive remain deferred.
